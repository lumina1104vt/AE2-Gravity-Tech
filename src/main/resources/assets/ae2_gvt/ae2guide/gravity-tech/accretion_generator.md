# Accretion Generator

<Row>
<BlockImage id="ae2_gvt:accretion_generator" scale="4" />
<RecipeFor id="ae2_gvt:accretion_generator" />
</Row>

Placed against a white hole, it generates from the hole's accretion: no fuel, and the hole does not shrink.

## How to use

- Cable it to the ME network and put it against a white hole (any of the six sides). A black hole gives nothing.
- Output: 1,000,000 FE/t.
- One white hole feeds 4 generators in full; with more, each one next to it gets proportionally less.

Its screen shows how many white holes it touches.

Tip: it is made in the [fusion generator](multiblocks/fusion-generator.md).
