The metals of Gravity Tech, used in armor, hub units and machine parts.

## How to use

- Alloy dust is turned into ingots in the [Gravity Processing Factory](../gravity_processing_factory.md).
- Alloy plates are made from the three ingots together.

Tip: the [fusion generator](../multiblocks/fusion-generator.md) has bulk recipes that turn a few ingots and plates into many.
