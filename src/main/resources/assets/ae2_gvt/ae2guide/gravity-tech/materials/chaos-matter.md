# Chaos Matter and Singularities

<Row>
<ItemImage id="ae2_gvt:chaos_matter" scale="3" /><ItemImage id="ae2_gvt:anti_singularity" scale="3" /><ItemImage id="ae2_gvt:paradox_quantum_entangled_singularity" scale="3" />

Three materials that are made in the world.

| Material | How to get it |
| :--- | :--- |
| Chaos matter | Blow up a black or white hole |
| Anti-singularity | Drop an AE2 singularity onto a black hole |
| Paradox quantum entangled singularity | Throw an anti-singularity, a singularity and paradox crystal dust within 1.5 blocks of each other and blow them up: 2 per set |

## How to use

- [Black and white holes](../holes.md) are remade from chaos matter.
- Chaos matter is also the catalyst of the [Gravity Processing Factory](../gravity_processing_factory.md).

Tip: the fusion generator can multiply chaos matter you already have.
