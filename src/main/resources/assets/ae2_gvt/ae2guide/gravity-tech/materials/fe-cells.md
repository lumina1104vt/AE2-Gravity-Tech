Keep FE in an ME network, as a resource of its own.

## How to use

Put the cell into an ME drive. It holds only FE. Sizes: 1K, 4K, 16K, 64K and 256K.

Tip: with Applied Flux installed, use its cells instead; these are hidden and have no recipe.
