# Module Cards

Cards for the [antigravity armor](armor.md), by the piece they go into. They are made in the [Gravity Processing Factory](gravity_processing_factory.md).

## Anti-gravity Helmet

* [Reach Module](armor_module_reach.md) — Reach blocks and creatures 3 blocks farther.
* [Pick Craft Module](armor_module_pick_craft.md) — Middle click a block to take a stack of it from the ME network. If there is none but a pattern for it, AE2's order screen opens.
* [Water Breathing Module](armor_module_water_breathing.md) — Breathe under water.
* [Luck Module](armor_module_luck.md) — Luck +2.
* [Night Vision Module](armor_module_night_vision.md) — Keeps Night Vision on you.
* [Auto Feeding Module](armor_module_auto_feed.md) — When hunger drops to 14 (7 drumsticks) or less, eats food from your inventory.
* [Saturation Module](armor_module_saturation.md) — Fills 1 hunger point every second until full. No food used.

## Anti-gravity Chestplate

* [Magnet Module](armor_module_magnet.md) — Pulls dropped items and experience within 8 blocks to you.
* [Auto Restock Module](armor_module_auto_stock.md) — When a stack in the hotbar or off hand drops to half or less, it is filled up from the ME network (0.2 AE per item).
* [Portable Workbench Module](armor_module_portable_workbench.md) — Press B to open a 3×3 crafting table anywhere.
* [Recharging Module](armor_module_recharge.md) — Every second, charges the items you carry from the network's power (up to 20,000 FE each, 2 FE per AE).
* [Fire Resistance Module](armor_module_fire_resistance.md) — Keeps the Fire Resistance effect on you.
* [Lava Immunity Module](armor_module_lava_immunity.md) — No damage from lava or fire, and you stop burning at once.
* [Max Health Module](armor_module_max_health.md) — Max health +8 (four hearts).
* [Regeneration Module](armor_module_regeneration.md) — Keeps Regeneration I on you.
* [Strength Module](armor_module_strength.md) — Attack damage +3.
* [Attack Speed Module](armor_module_attack_speed.md) — Attack speed +1.
* [Damage Reduction Module](armor_module_damage_reduction.md) — Every hit does 3 less damage.
* [Thorns Module](armor_module_thorns.md) — Whoever hits you in melee takes 4 damage back.
* [Undying Module](armor_module_undying.md) — A deadly hit leaves you with half your health instead, plus a short Regeneration and Absorption. Once every 2 minutes.
* [Shield Module](armor_module_shield.md) — When your golden hearts are gone, gives Absorption II (four golden hearts) again, checked every 10 seconds.
* [Cleansing Module](armor_module_cleanse.md) — Removes harmful effects as they come.
* [Dig Affinity Module](armor_module_dig_affinity.md) — Mining is no slower in the air or under water.

## Anti-gravity Leggings

* [Movement Speed Module](armor_module_move_speed.md) — Walk 30% faster.
* [Swim Speed Module](armor_module_swim_speed.md) — Swim 50% faster.
* [Phase Module](armor_module_phase.md) — While flying you pass through blocks.
* [Evasion Module](armor_module_evasion.md) — A hit has a 15% chance to do nothing.

## Anti-gravity Boots

* [Jump Height Module](armor_module_jump_height.md) — Jump higher (jump strength +0.25; a normal jump is 0.42).
* [Step Assist Module](armor_module_step_assist.md) — Walk straight up a one block high step without jumping.
* [Flight Anti-Drift Module](armor_module_flight_drift.md) — While flying, letting go of the movement keys stops you quickly instead of gliding on.
* [Dash Module](armor_module_dash.md) — Press V to dash the way you are looking, once every 1.5 seconds.
