# Annihilation TNT

<Row>
<ItemImage id="ae2_gvt:annihilation_tnt" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Dangerous.** It does not explode like TNT: it annihilates everything in a sphere of radius 64, **bedrock included**, and nothing drops.

## What happens

- The sphere is erased from the centre outward over a few seconds.
- Anything alive inside is struck: a totem of undying saves a player, armor does not. The shockwave hurts out to 128 blocks.
- Black and white holes caught in it are destroyed **without** leaving chaos matter.
- A permanent, unbreakable **space rift** stays at the centre.

## Space rift

Walk into it to reach the Shattered Starfield, a dimension of its own. A matching rift there leads back.

Tip: do not light it anywhere near your base.
