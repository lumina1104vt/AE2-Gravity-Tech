What gives the [twin-star hub](twin-star-hub.md) its abilities.

## How to use

- Start from the blank unit, which is the base of the others.
- Put a **black hole crafting unit** into the hub's black slot for crafting, and a **white hole computing unit** into the white slot for crafting CPUs.
- The tiers are Singular, Imaginary, Paradox and Planck; the numbers are in the table on the [hub page](twin-star-hub.md).

Tip: upgrade by swapping the unit; the structure stays.
