# Crystal Dust Generator

The most basic power source. Connect it to the ME network and it feeds power into it. It burns crystal dust.

## How to use

- Connect it to the ME network (a cable touching it).
- Put gravity, anti-gravity or paradox dust in: right-click to open the window and fill the slot, right-click it with dust in your hand, or insert with a hopper or pipe.
- While the network has power, it also pulls this dust from the network by itself.

## Power output

| Dust | Output | Burn time |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity | 2,000 FE/t | 2 s |
| Anti-gravity | 2,000 FE/t | 2 s |
| Paradox | 4,000 FE/t | 2 s |

It can be started by hand-feeding dust when the network has no power, which is how you power machines like the Graviton Collector at first.
