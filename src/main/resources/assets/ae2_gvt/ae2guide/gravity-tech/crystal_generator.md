# Crystal Dust Generator

<Row>
<BlockImage id="ae2_gvt:crystal_generator" scale="4" />
<RecipeFor id="ae2_gvt:crystal_generator" />
</Row>

The most basic generator: it burns crystal dust and puts the power into the ME network.

## How to use

- Cable it to the ME network.
- Put gravity, anti-gravity or paradox crystal dust in: through its screen, by right clicking it with the dust, or with a hopper or pipe.
- While the network has power, it also takes dust from the network by itself.

## Output

| Dust | Output | Burn time |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Gravity | 2,000 FE/t | 2 s |
| Anti-gravity | 2,000 FE/t | 2 s |
| Paradox | 4,000 FE/t | 2 s |

Tip: with an unpowered network, put dust in by hand to start it, then use it to power the graviton collectors.
