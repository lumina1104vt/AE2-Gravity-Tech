# Gravity Constrainer

<RecipeFor id="ae2_gvt:gravity_constrainer" />
</Row>

Holds the plasma in the [fusion generator](multiblocks/fusion-generator.md).

## How to use

The chamber is a 9×9×9 cube with a 7×7×7 inside:

| Where | What goes there |
| :--- | :--- |
| The 12 edges, corners included | Structure frame |
| The faces | Containment glass or structure frame, with at least one structure port; energy ports as wanted |
| The middle of one of the four side walls | Fusion controller, facing out |
| Inside, against the middle of each of the six walls | **Six gravity constrainers** |
| The rest of the inside | Empty |

Tip: the constrainers can be placed facing any way; they turn to the centre when the structure forms.
