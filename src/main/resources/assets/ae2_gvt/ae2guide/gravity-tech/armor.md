# Antigravity Armor

## Full set

With all four pieces on you can **fly** (as in creative) and take **no fall damage**.

Flying takes **1 anti-graviton every 5 ticks** (4 a second) from the linked network. With none left you drop out of the air.

## Linking to a network

Put a piece into the link slot of an AE2 **wireless access point**, as you would a wireless terminal. One linked piece is enough, and by default you can use it from anywhere, in any dimension, while the access point is loaded.

## Module cards

Wear the armor and press **K**: choose a piece, put a card into the slot to install it, and switch it off or take it out there.
