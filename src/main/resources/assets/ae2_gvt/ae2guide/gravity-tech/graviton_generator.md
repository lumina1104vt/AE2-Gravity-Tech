# Graviton Generator

<Row>
<BlockImage id="ae2_gvt:graviton_generator" scale="4" />
<RecipeFor id="ae2_gvt:graviton_generator" />
</Row>

Generates power from the gravitons or anti-gravitons stored in the ME network.

## How to use

- Cable it to an ME network that holds gravitons (see [Gravitons and anti-gravitons](materials/gravitons.md)).
- It takes 1 a second and gives 200,000 FE/t.
- The button on the left of its screen chooses what it burns: either, gravitons only, or anti-gravitons only.

Tip: the network needs power before gravitons can be taken out, so have another source (such as the crystal dust generator) to start it.
